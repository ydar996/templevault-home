Features

Member Directory

Every person in your church gets a profile: contact details, family links, tags, giving history, and notes. Members update their own information in the Member Portal, and ushers can register people at the door - including existing members who were never added.

Giving and Bank Reconciliation

  • Record gifts by cash, check, card, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, or platform payout.
  • Import bank statements and post deposits to the right book with one click.
  • Stripe card fees post as expenses automatically - gross gift credited to the donor, net matched to the deposit.
  • The Bank vs Book check flags any line that does not add up, to the cent.
  • Duplicate imports and verification deposits are detected and ignored safely.

Attendance and Usher Portal

Ushers log in to their member portal to take attendance by QR scan or name search, add and edit member records, and attach children to parent accounts - all scoped to the usher tag.

Platform Settlements

Zeffy, Cash App, and Venmo detail files reconcile against bank remittances. Each gift lands on the right donor and the right fund, with referral credit where it belongs.

Text-to-Give

Donors give by SMS or secure checkout link. Text-to-give pledges settle into gifts the moment they clear, and SMS costs are metered per church.

Reports and Audit

Giving statements, category breakdowns, and an audit log on every mutation keep your finance records defensible.
