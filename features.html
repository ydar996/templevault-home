Features

Member Directory

Every person in your church gets a profile: contact details, family links, tags, giving history, and notes. Member numbers are church-scoped (TV-…), bulk import accepts Excel or CSV, and statuses cover Active, Prospect, Unconfirmed, Separated, and missed-registration. Members update their own information in the Member Portal, and ushers can add and edit people at the door.

Onboarding and Visitor Follow-Up

A public join link and QR code turn visitors into members instantly, with a portal login emailed and WhatsApped to them. Assign an Onboarding Lead and team - every new join creates follow-up tasks, a documented first contact, and a second outreach 14 days later. Group leaders complete missing member details for their own tags.

The Member Portal

  • Give by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or Zeffy - with pending-confirmation tracking until the money lands.
  • My Giving history with downloadable PDF giving statements, personal or family.
  • My Family household view - members add relatives after a directory check that prevents duplicates.
  • Events with programs, announcements, in-app messages, pastor appointment booking, and referral credit.

Giving and Bank Reconciliation

  • Record gifts by cash, check, card, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or platform payout - anonymous, guest, and member gifts all supported.
  • Import bank statements and post deposits to the right book with one click. Statement name and Zelle-alias matching runs before fuzzy name match.
  • Stripe card fees post as expenses automatically - gross gift credited to the donor, net matched to the deposit.
  • Duplicate imports, platform verification deposits, and CD maturity returns are detected and handled safely.
  • The Bank vs Book check flags any line that does not add up, to the cent.

Platform Settlements

Zeffy, Cash App, Venmo, and PayPal detail files reconcile against bank remittances. Each gift lands on the right donor and the right fund, card-fee handling balances to the cent, and referral credit goes where it belongs.

Church Accounting

  • GAAP chart of accounts with denomination packs and per-fund GL mapping.
  • Monthly budgets, journals, vendors with 1099 totals, and recurring bills that post when due.
  • Accounting reports with GL drill-down, balance sheet, and cash-versus-investment positions.
  • Calendar-year period close, Form 990 workpaper pack, and a one-click auditor export.

Attendance and Usher Portal

Ushers log in to their member portal to take attendance by QR scan or name search, add and edit member records, attach children to parents, and read submitted testimonies - all scoped to the usher tag. Events support recurring series, per-date programs, cover images, and bulk QR sheets.

Camera Check-In (IntelliScan)

Churches with door-camera hardware can plug in the IntelliScan101 partner integration: recognized active adults are marked present automatically, one check-in per person per event no matter how they were captured, and unknown faces alert staff without creating member records. Minors, inactive members, and do-not-match profiles are never matchable.

Attendance Insights

Trends, visitor retention, no-show analysis, event comparison, and export tools show who is coming, who stopped, and who needs a follow-up visit.

Pastoral Care

Action Items with dashboards, recurring tasks, templates, and team workload views keep care work visible. Pastoral notes live on the member profile, and custom fields shape each task type. Birthday and anniversary outreach can send a pastor-signed note automatically.

Groups, Structure, and Treasuries

Tags group members for ministry cohorts and outreach. Organograms map your organization, ministries, and committees with vacant seats and succession pipelines - every tagged group gets its own EXCO chart. Group treasuries keep fellowship purses separate from church giving while EXCO seats record dues and events from the portal.

Forms, Letters, and Appointments

Staff build forms and letters, collect responses, and book appointments without leaving TempleVault. Public form and booking links work for members and visitors, and new people from form answers arrive as Unconfirmed directory rows - never duplicates.

Communications

Reach members where they already read: SMS and WhatsApp broadcasts to enrolled members, a full Email Broadcast composer, and in-app member messaging with subjects and attachments. A short welcome goes out on every join, signed by your lead pastors.

Insights and Reports

Demographics by age, gender, marital status, and geography. Giving insights by household, fund, destination book, channel, and tender. Upcoming birthdays, giving statements, and Excel/CSV exports for the staff who need them.

Registers

Baby dedications, a marriage register with former-name history, and a testimonies queue with present mode keep your church records and shared moments in one place.

Security and Roles

MFA, forced password change, 30-minute idle logout, and a security activity log protect congregation data. A role permissions matrix lets lead pastors shape access for pastoral seats like Assistant Pastor, Deacon, Executive Pastor, Campus Pastor, and Elder. Each signed Church Services Agreement is stored on the church.
