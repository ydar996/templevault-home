Everything your church runs on

One system for the front door and the back office. Members, families, giving, and services stay connected - no spreadsheets between them.

Member Directory

Profiles, families, tags, and a portal where members manage their own details. Ushers can register walk-ins and link children to parents on the spot.

Giving and Reconciliation

Record every gift, import bank statements, and reconcile to the cent. Stripe fees, Zeffy, Cash App, and Venmo settlements land on the right book automatically.

Attendance and Usher Tools

Sunday check-in by QR code or name search. Ushers take attendance, update member records, and welcome first-time guests from their phones.

Family and Household Giving

Link spouses, children, and households. Family giving statements and tax records cover the whole household.

Text-to-Give

Members give by SMS or card - Apple Pay, Google Pay, and checkout links all reconcile to the same donor record.

Reports and Audit Trail

Giving statements, bank-vs-book checks, and a full audit log on every record change. Your finance team sees exactly what moved and when.
