Everything your church runs on

One system for the front door and the back office. Members, families, giving, and services stay connected - no spreadsheets between them.

Member Directory

Profiles, families, tags, church-scoped member numbers, and a portal where members manage their own details. Ushers can register walk-ins and link children to parents on the spot.

Giving and Reconciliation

Record every gift, import bank statements, and reconcile to the cent. Stripe fees and US platform settlements (Zeffy, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal) land on the right book automatically.

Attendance and Usher Tools

Sunday check-in by QR code or name search. Ushers take attendance, update member records, and welcome guests from their phones - with retention and trend analytics behind it all.

Every Way People Give

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and text-to-give - US churches also take Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, and Zeffy. Every gift reconciles to the same donor record.

Church Accounting

A GAAP chart of accounts, monthly budgets, vendors and 1099s, journals, and reports with drill-down - plus a period close and auditor export.

Pastoral Care and Portal

Action items, onboarding follow-ups, pastoral notes, and a member portal with giving statements, family management, messages, and event RSVPs.

Books that match the bank. Every time.

Every posted bank line links to a real ledger entry - gifts, fees, transfers. The Bank vs Book check names any drift in dollars and cents instead of leaving you guessing.
